### Refined page priorities

The planner confirms a strong local structure, but it does not contain search-volume, competition, CPC or intent values. The current priority order is therefore based on commercial clarity and local relevance rather than a claimed ranking forecast.

1. **Home:** `Joseph's Mystic Herbs Fish Hoek` as the branded entity phrase, supported by `traditional healer Fish Hoek` and `herbal remedies Fish Hoek`.
2. **Herbs:** `mountain herbs Fish Hoek`, supported by `buchu and sage Cape Town`, `herb bundles Fish Hoek` and `Wilde Dagga herbs`.
3. **Goods:** `herbal balms and ointments Fish Hoek`, supported by `natural soap Fish Hoek`, named preparations and prices.
4. **About:** `traditional healer Cape Town`, supported by the supplied Joseph story and Fish Hoek location.
5. **Contact:** `contact traditional healer Fish Hoek`, supported by Shoprite, Dunster Road, phone, email and WhatsApp terms.
6. **Journal:** `mountain herbs journal`, supported by articles around buchu, sage, bundles, local gathering and ordering.

### Local landing-page opportunities

The highest-value local landing page is the existing Contact page because it combines the location, map, direct phone number, email, WhatsApp route and collection context. The homepage should remain the entity page, while the Herbs page should own the product discovery language. The Goods page should own price and preparation searches.

Each page should link to the others with local, descriptive anchor text. Examples include **view mountain herbs in Fish Hoek**, **see herbal balm prices**, **read Joseph's traditional healer story** and **find Joseph at the Dunster Road Shoprite entrance**.

### Planner data required for a ranking forecast

The current planner file is a seed list rather than a metrics export. Before making claims about opportunity size or competitiveness, add monthly searches, trend, competition, CPC, country, language and intent for South Africa / Cape Town / Fish Hoek variants. Also add close variants such as `herbalist Fish Hoek`, `herbal remedies near me Fish Hoek`, `sage bundle Cape Town`, `buchu Fish Hoek`, `natural soap Cape Town` and `traditional healer near me Cape Town`.

After those fields are available, group terms by intent, assign one primary phrase per page, identify cannibalisation risks and prioritise the first three Journal articles by demand and achievable local relevance.
