Meet Joseph

Traditional knowledge.
Rooted in Cape Town.

Joseph is a Cape Town traditional healer trained by Khoi San elders. His work follows a Rastafarian way of life grounded in respect for nature, spiritual connection, self-reliance, community, natural living and the steady rhythm of the land.

He travels into the mountains, fields and herb beds to gather plants such as Wilde Dagga, mountain garlic, aloe, African potato, sage and buchu. He prepares balms, ointments, soaps and other herbal goods by hand, with the ingredients and current availability explained clearly.
